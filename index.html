📚 How to Plan Your Home Loan

1. Assess Your Affordability

Your EMI should not exceed 40-50% of your monthly income. Use our calculator to find the right loan amount and tenure.

2. Compare Interest Rates

Even 0.5% difference can save lakhs over the loan tenure. Research and negotiate for the best rate.

3. Understand Pre-EMI (CLP)

For under-construction properties, you pay interest only on disbursed amounts. Plan for these pre-EMI payments.

4. Make Prepayments Wisely

Use bonuses, tax refunds, or savings for prepayments. "Reduce Tenure" saves more interest than "Reduce EMI".

5. Choose the Right Tenure

Longer tenure = lower EMI but higher total interest. Shorter tenure = higher EMI but significant savings.

6. Consider Floating Rates

Floating rates may be lower initially but can increase. Use our rate change feature to see the impact.

7. Build Emergency Fund

Keep 3-6 months of EMI as emergency fund before taking the loan. This ensures you can pay during financial crunches.

8. Save Scenarios

Use our Save feature to compare different loan options. This helps you make an informed decision.
